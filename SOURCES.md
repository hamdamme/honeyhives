# Sources

Every reported number in the initial dataset points to its issuing source.

- [Honey, March 13, 2026](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Publications/Todays_Reports/reports/hony0326.pdf), USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service. Reports 2025 United States honey production, yield per producing colony, and change from 2024.
- [Bee and Honey surveys and reports](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Surveys/Guide_to_NASS_Surveys/Bee_and_Honey/index.php), USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service. Describes the agency's honey and colony reporting program.
- [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/), Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations. Official international food and agriculture statistics. Use the selected domain, item, element, unit, and reporting year when adding country comparisons.

The records currently include United States figures only. No international order is stated until comparable country observations are added from FAOSTAT or another official source. Keep the downloaded source at its original site; this repository stores cited values and code, not source reports.
